# Sector photo credits

Stock photos used on the sector pages (`/setores`, `/setores/[setor]`) and the
home "Soluções por setor" band. Every photo is under a free licence that allows
commercial use without attribution (Unsplash License / Pexels License); this
file is the record of where each one came from. The licence was checked on each
photo's own page (no Unsplash+, premium, iStock or sponsored images).

Each source was downloaded at 2400 px from the site's image CDN, then cropped
to 3:2 and resized to 1600×1067 (`<slug>.webp`, WebP q78, metadata stripped)
plus a 1200×630 Open Graph crop (`og/<slug>.jpg`, JPEG q78). Full-size
originals are not kept in the repo.
